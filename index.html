The origin

An exceptional wood,
a millennia-old
craftsmanship

Teak is one of the most precious woods in the world, recognized for its density, natural resistance to humidity, insects and weather. Maison Exotik rigorously selects each piece from master artisans who carry on centuries-old wood-carving traditions — guaranteeing authenticity and longevity.

Grade A Teak

Our teak is harvested from certified plantations, ensuring exceptional density and longevity.

Hand-carved

Every motif is chiseled by hand by artisans trained from childhood in centuries-old wood-carving traditions.

Direct import

Maison Exotik selects each piece directly from the workshops — no middlemen, to preserve quality.

Green commitment

Responsible commerce,
preserved planet

Choosing Maison Exotik means choosing furniture that respects the forest and supports a sustainable green economy. Our environmental commitment is at the heart of every business decision.

Certified Forests

All our teak comes from sustainably managed plantations, certified by independent forestry-control organizations. No primary forest is exploited.

Green Economy

We actively contribute to the green economy by supporting human-scale artisan workshops, promoting fair working conditions and ethical commerce.

Mindful Transport

Our maritime freight containers are optimized to minimize the carbon footprint. We favor grouped deliveries and recyclable packaging.

The eco-tax
for a green future

In France and on Réunion Island, an eco-tax is collected on new imported furniture. This contribution — included in our prices — is paid to the eco-organization Éco-mobilier, which finances the collection, sorting and recycling of end-of-life furniture across the territory.

At Maison Exotik, we don't just comply with this obligation: we embrace it. It's our concrete way of taking part in the circular economy and offering you a fully responsible purchase.
